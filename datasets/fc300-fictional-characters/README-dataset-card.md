# FC300 — Black Fictional Characters Registry
### CTL-DS-FC3-000001 · v1.0 package (pre-publication) · CultureTechLens NFP

**Cite as:** CultureTechLens, "FC300 — Black Fictional Characters Registry (CTL-DS-FC3-000001)", v1.0, 2026. DOI assigned at Zenodo deposit (founder step, pending).

**What it is:** 300 Black fictional characters — TV 70 · Film 60 · Comics 50 · Animation 40 · Books 40 · Games 25 · Stage 15 — each with creator, first appearance, portrayer, citation, and a full AEO layer. Sister registry to KG333 (real people & places); 167 of its rows also appear in AEO-500.

**The finding:** only 139 of the 300 have a Wikidata record of their own; **157 (52%) exist solely inside their franchise's page**. This registry is where they stand alone.

**Status:** v1.0 package assembled 2026-11-16 under the dataset spec of the same date; rulings by William Maxey 2026-11-16 (ID, canonical CSV, Coverage Status verbatim, images never ship). License on publication: CC BY 4.0 on the registry metadata (open core, CTL-TERMS-001).

**Known limits:** verification is a 2026-11-04 snapshot · page-slug liveness unverified (site lane) · creator/portrayer blanks are recorded gaps · no images are distributed, ever (spec R3).
