## Citation Status — reader crosswalk (a reporting aid, NOT a CTL grade)
This dataset's evidence vocabulary is Citation Status, kept verbatim (spec §3). In plain language: **External — Wikipedia / Wikidata** (321) = the answer is backed by the subject's encyclopedia article and Wikidata item, both checked 2026-11-04. **Own Wikipedia Article** (59) / **Wikidata Item — No Own Wikipedia Article** (18) = one of the two external anchors present. **Franchise Coverage Only — No Character QID Found** (88) = fictional characters documented through their franchise, with no character-level Wikidata item. **GAP — CTL source of record, no external page** (11) = no external page exists; CultureTechLens' own record is the citation — the dataset's owned-evidence class. **Partial Gap** (2) = article without a Wikidata item. Do not convert these statuses into CTL claim grades; they describe citation coverage, not claim verification.
