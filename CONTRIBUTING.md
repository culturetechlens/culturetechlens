# Contributing to CultureTechLens

CultureTechLens (CTL) is a Chicago-based Black cultural intelligence archive.
Its promise is simple: every claim graded, every source named, every
correction public. Contributions are welcome when they keep that promise.

## The fastest way to help: report an error

Found a wrong date, name, number, or credit? Open an issue using the
**Report an error** form. Include the record ID, what is wrong, and the
evidence. Verified reports are published in the
[corrections log](https://culturetechlens.com/about/corrections/) with the
original text, the corrected text, and the date. We do not silently edit.
Reporters are credited if they wish.

## Other ways to contribute

- **Source suggestions:** a primary document (filing, deed, photograph
  caption card, newspaper page) that supports or contradicts a record.
- **Missing records:** a person, place, organization, work, event, or
  practice that belongs in the graph, with at least one named source.
- **Documentation and tooling:** fixes to README, DATA-DICTIONARY,
  schemas, or scripts in `tools/`.

## Evidence standards

- Every claim needs a named source. A record without a source does not
  enter the graph.
- Prefer primary sources (documents, filings, archives, contemporaneous
  reporting) over summaries.
- Do not cite Wikipedia as a source for a graded claim. It can help you
  find sources.
- Grades follow the CTL methodology in `methodology/` (CTL-MTH-001). Do not
  assign a grade in a pull request; propose evidence and the maintainers
  stamp it.
- Unknowns are never reported as zero.

## Pull requests

1. Open an issue first for anything beyond a typo fix.
2. One topic per pull request.
3. Describe the change, link the evidence, and list the record IDs affected.
4. Do not edit generated counts by hand. `counts.json` is regenerated by
   the scripts in `tools/`.
5. Maintainers may request changes. Accepted changes that alter published
   data are logged in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Licensing of contributions

By contributing, you agree that your contributions are licensed under the
same terms as the repository: CC BY 4.0 for content and MIT for code. See
`LICENSE` and `LICENSES.md`.

## Conduct

Be accurate, be respectful, and assume good faith. This archive documents
real communities and real people. Harassment, personal attacks, and
fabricated sourcing are grounds for removal from the project.

## Response times

We aim to acknowledge error reports within 5 business days. Verified
corrections are published in the corrections log within 10 business days
of verification.
