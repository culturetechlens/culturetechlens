# Code of Conduct

CultureTechLens (CTL) is a Chicago-based Black cultural intelligence archive.
Its promise is simple: every claim graded, every source named, every
correction public. This code keeps the spaces where that work happens —
issues, pull requests, discussions — accurate, respectful, and safe for the
communities the archive documents.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being accurate: citing sources, correcting the record in public, and
  distinguishing fact from inference.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, experiences, and communities —
  this archive documents real people and real neighborhoods.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback about the work.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and logging the correction.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or derogatory language toward any person
  or community.
- Fabricated or misrepresented sourcing.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to **info@culturetechlens.com**. Reports are
reviewed by the maintainers. Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or
contributions that violate this code, and may restrict participation for
repeated or severe violations. Where a reported error in the record is the
real issue, use the **Report an error** issue form instead — factual
corrections go to the public corrections log, not to conduct review.
