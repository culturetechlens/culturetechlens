# CultureTechLens — Schema & Documentation

**Culture, Clearly Seen.** · Black Cultural Intelligence

The machine-readable heart of CultureTechLens, documented in one place.

## The knowledge graph

- **Schema specification:** [CTL-KG-001-SPEC](../knowledge-graph/schema/CTL-KG-001-SPEC-knowledge-graph-schema.md) — the eight record types (Person, Work, Place, Event, Institution, Outlet, Topic, List), the relationship vocabulary, and the evidence-grading rules.
- **Schema (JSON):** [CTL-KG-001-schema.json](../knowledge-graph/schema/CTL-KG-001-schema.json)
- **Data dictionary:** [DATA-DICTIONARY.md](../DATA-DICTIONARY.md) — every field in the published CSVs, defined.
- **The data:** [`knowledge-graph/entities.csv`](../knowledge-graph/entities.csv) · [`knowledge-graph/edges.csv`](../knowledge-graph/edges.csv) — the stamped slice: 3,488 entities, 2,162 relationships, every record graded VERIFIED or STRONG EVIDENCE.
- **Samples:** [`samples/`](../samples/) — 100 records as CSV and JSON-LD for testing and teaching.
- **Counts:** [`counts.json`](../counts.json) — generated, never hand-edited (CTL-MTH-004), and verified in CI on every change.
